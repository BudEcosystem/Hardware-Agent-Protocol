# Feedback, value maps and physical workflow execution

Independent HMS design analysis, 2026-11-06. Inner Monologue and VoxPoser add two useful mechanisms to the earlier [planning analysis](planning-and-grounding.md): revising a plan from environmental feedback and generating spatial objectives for a local motion planner. Neither mechanism supplies a durable physical operation contract by itself. The proposals here extend [execution semantics](../spec/02-execution.md); this document does not implement or qualify a hardware planner.

Reading scope is explicit: complete extracted text of Inner Monologue arXiv v1, its published main paper and separate supplement, and VoxPoser arXiv v2; all numbered figures and tables in those documents; six official metadata/project pages; and ten complete selected files from the pinned VoxPoser repository. The [receipt](../research/planning/reading-continuation-full.json) covers 7,306 LF-delimited lines in 44 exact chunks. Seventy-three PDF page renders are retained, with 31 substantive pages visually inspected. This is not a claim that every prose page was separately inspected as an image, that videos were watched, or that either entire repository was reviewed.

As checked on 2026-11-06, Inner Monologue has one arXiv version, submitted 2022-07-12, and a CoRL 2022 publication in PMLR 205 (2023), pages 1769–1782. VoxPoser has arXiv v2, revised 2023-11-02, and a CoRL 2023 publication in PMLR 229, pages 540–562. The acquired VoxPoser paper is v2; the proceedings metadata was checked, but a second proceedings PDF was not acquired. [Inner Monologue version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.05608), [publication](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v205/huang23c.html), [VoxPoser version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.05973), [publication](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v229/huang23b.html).

Inner Monologue routes success reports, scene descriptions and human answers back into skill selection. Evidence differs across its settings: simulated tabletop feedback uses simulator state; real tabletop perception uses MDETR; the kitchen experiment uses human-provided object feedback and a learned success detector. Reported real tabletop combined-feedback success is 100% stacking and 80% sorting over ten trials each; the kitchen aggregate is 60.4%, compared with 30.8% for its baseline. These results concern particular skill libraries and feedback sources. They do not measure durable request identity, restart recovery or stop latency. [Published main paper, sections 3–4 and Tables 2–3](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v205/huang23c/huang23c.pdf).

The published supplement materially qualifies transfer to HMS: success thresholds vary by task; toy fruits and bottles are rigid, smaller and lighter than their counterparts; and the kitchen detector combines a thresholded foresight score with a hindsight classification. A numeric detector output needs a named event and domain, not an assumed universal probability of safe completion. Its tabletop model is identified as `text-davinci-002`; arXiv v1 instead identifies InstructGPT-1.3B. The main paper specifies injected action noise of 4 mm capped at 2σ, whereas both supplements describe 1.5 cm for stacking and 0.7 cm for sorting, capped at 1.5σ. That discrepancy remains unresolved. The published supplement also has visibly unresolved citation/cross-reference markers. Hidden color-scheme/placeholder text in PDF extraction is obscured by actual figure artwork and should not be treated as a visible missing figure. [Published supplement, A.1–A.3 and B.2](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v205/huang23c/huang23c-supp.pdf), [older preprint, Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.05608v1).

VoxPoser turns generated functions into 3D value maps and replans from perception. Its five quantified real-world tasks have 44/50 successes in static conditions and 35/50 under disturbances; the larger simulation benchmark covers 13 tasks. Rapid online learning for doors, windows and fridges is simulation-only, over three seeds. The paper explicitly limits planning to end-effector trajectories and identifies perception and contact-rich dynamics as weaknesses. Avoidance is a weighted objective, not a proof that excluded space is unreachable. Its half-open-drawer example first opens the drawer fully: a requested final state does not automatically constrain every intermediate state. [VoxPoser v2, sections 3–5, Tables 1–3 and Appendix A.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.05973v2).

The evidence therefore supports different conclusions at different layers:

| Evidence available | Useful conclusion | HMS question still requiring independent evidence |
|---|---|---|
| A planner revises its next skill after feedback | Feedback can improve task continuation | Was the last effect committed, partly committed, or merely reported? |
| A rendered map and successful trajectory | A spatial proposal can be useful for a particular scene | Were tool, payload, whole-arm motion and all forbidden regions checked? |
| A simulator success/reward signal | The stated simulation criterion was satisfied | Does the criterion correspond to the physical deployment and its measurement uncertainty? |
| A human changes a natural-language goal | The planner can interpret a changed request | Who may change the goal, which active work must stop, and when is the new authority effective? |

No acquired code, notebook, simulator or model client was executed. Other repository files, dependencies and the externally linked experimental prompts remain outside this review. In particular, the two RLBench prompt files above must not be described as all real-world experimental prompts.

For HMS, feedback should be an evidence envelope before it becomes planner prose. Proposed fields include source identity, observation sequence, observation and receipt times, clock uncertainty, entity/material identity, frame and calibration revision, estimator version, validity window, uncertainty, and evidence references. Keep `commanded`, `reported`, `estimated` and `independently_verified` meanings distinct. A successful request response, cached desired state and visual classifier score answer different questions. If the target is occluded or identity is ambiguous, represent that uncertainty explicitly rather than carrying the previous label forward as fact.

A task-progress predicate should bind its evaluator version, required inputs, coordinate frame and tolerance. Evaluation of a planner-generated subgoal can report whether that subgoal holds while the overall user goal is still wrong. Maintain the accepted goal and its evidence requirements independently of the planner's proposed decomposition. Detected regression must reopen the affected subgoal. Evidence can become stale even while an operation remains terminal: another actor may move the object, alter a thermostat or replace a sample.

A value map should be a proposal artifact. Record its hash, grid dimensions and resolution, frame/calibration binding, observation revision, object bindings, generation/model version and intended effect. Separate soft preferences from hard conditions. The latter include installed workspace limits, whole-arm/tool/payload geometry, contact/force limits, reserved shared zones, maximum speed and cumulative travel. A low cost at a voxel cannot authorize entry into a forbidden region. Unknown geometry and unobserved obstacles require a specified local policy, not an implicit zero-cost region.

Admission should bind the immutable procedure, approved envelope, resource claims, endpoint session and fence, together with the relevant observation revisions. The controller must independently validate the exact primitive arguments and currently applicable limits before effects. Recheck assumptions that can change during motion at a qualified local rate. Replanning may change a trajectory within an already authorized bounded primitive; changing its object, contact mode, physical budget or affected resources needs the corresponding new admission. A controller's local interpolation loop should not depend on a cloud model responding to each waypoint.

Generated computation and physical execution need different failure rules. Isolate map generation from drivers, credentials and mutable controller objects. Apply memory, CPU, synthesis and model-call limits to computation; apply travel, energy, force, material, time and repetition limits where effects occur. The latter must persist through network retries. A cached language-model response can avoid another synthesis request, but it neither deduplicates motion nor establishes that cached geometry is current. Reusing a code artifact after restart still requires current context and effect reconciliation.

Explicit endpoint behavior is needed for loss of perception, planner outage, lease expiry, authority revocation and conflicting manual activity. Define whether the local primitive may finish, hold, retract or stop, and the evidence required to transition out of that state. Holding can itself consume energy or damage a specimen; releasing a gripper can create a new hazard. The correct fallback is deployment-specific. Record measured stop behavior and residual queued motion rather than deriving a physical stop guarantee from a model choosing a termination token.

Goal changes should create traceable revisions under an authorized principal. A sentence inserted into a perception stream is evidence to interpret, not authority to widen scope. Before replacing active work, reconcile its executed prefix and any partial effects. Operation IDs and immutable intent digests should preserve result lookup after lost replies; a new goal revision must not silently erase pending effects or reuse an old ID for a different intent.

Exploration deserves its own admitted scope. Asking for information does not by itself authorize moving an object, probing contact or consuming a sample. A proposed diagnostic action needs a hypothesis, expected information, physical bounds, stop conditions and allowed follow-up. The accepted goal should distinguish a final-state target from invariants that must hold throughout execution. If a drawer must never exceed half travel, a strategy that measures full travel first is inadmissible even when its final position would be correct.

Rollback is usually a new physical operation. A failed grasp may displace an object; opening a container may alter its contents; an inspection fixture may retain a part after the host loses its reply. A default-pose command cannot restore those states. Preserve the effect record, observe the current world and admit any compensation with its own identity, authority and preconditions. Where restoration is impossible, expose the irreversible result and unmet goal. The strongest atomic virtual-state/receipt guarantee in the HMS synthetic plant remains a simulation property.

Evaluation should measure task success, evidence quality and execution containment separately. Add scenarios for stale frame calibration, ambiguous object identity, occlusion after admission, changed obstacles after smoothing, a forbidden intermediate state, ignored speed parameters, arm failure followed by gripper activity, repeated model outages, lost operation replies and manual intervention. Preserve incomplete and unknown outcomes, not only successful demonstrations. Simulator tests can exercise protocol/state-machine logic; only deployment-specific commissioning and physical measurements can establish controller timing, stopping distance, uncertainty and safe operating limits.
