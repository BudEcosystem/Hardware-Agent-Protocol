# Retained-state audit

After the one candidate attempt terminates, join every start to its reaped
terminal receipt and verify frozen source/native/compiler/header identities and
all 244 public baseline files. Preserve the preceding seals and failed images.

Reuse the exact independent hierarchy reader with strictly read-only backing
descriptors and forbidden program/erase/sync callbacks. Reconstruct the 18
semantic images, two comparison images and any complete-workload image. Check
full bodies, namespaces, interruption/recovery lineage, per-step callbacks and
comparison chunk equality. Join completed progress to the original dataset and
inspect any partial published file without adapter recovery or repair.

Also open the seven new raw synchronization fixtures read-only under their
original object anchors and exclusive advisory locks. Verify metadata, owner,
manifest, allocation and all byte hashes before/after; inspect the explicitly
retained partial-write and failed-wait payloads. Never reopen a failed fixture
through the provider. Other original raw test fixtures remain in the final seal.

Compare actual synchronization call counts: the previous profile performs one
fsync per chunk per block sync; the candidate keeps one full fsync or error wait
per chunk, plus its two owner-admission wait probes in the paired screen. The
acknowledgement model is an ordering control, not a physical power-loss proof.

The audit child has 60 wall / 40 CPU seconds, 512 MiB address space, 128 FDs and
16 MiB per file. No SQLite, recovery, mount changes, physical I/O or public install.
Retain any audit failure; do not silently rerun an unchanged scope.
