The proposal addresses the complete physical/application gap through shared
private serialization and full-width time predicates across both handlers and
standing poll, followed by measured phase boundaries. It preserves the original
target, ABI, roots, storage, callback/capture/failure order, full uint64 domain and
loss/authority semantics. The private arithmetic leaves must preserve unsigned
modulo subtraction, strict/inclusive comparisons, sentinel policy and the exact
saturating-min expression. No savings are promised and no source implementation
or compiler invocation is included in this design packet.
