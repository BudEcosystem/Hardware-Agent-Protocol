and [timing/outcome design](../pat-io-timing-oracle-design/DESIGN.md). The current

25 fixed challenges. Complete group evidence is necessary; it does not authorize
automatic promotion or establish physical/deployment qualification.

an isolated library; no I/O supervisor launch or quota enforcement is claimed.

160 pure checks and one actual owned GPIO group. It verifies protected inputs
before launch, the fixed code image and generated attempt ID, complete transport,
independent inspection of all 30 cases, exact replay across reopen and same-key
conflict. This is evidence for the tested synthetic GPIO process path. Its archive
still reports no semantic qualification or hard aggregate enforcement.
