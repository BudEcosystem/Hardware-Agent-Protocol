and [timing/outcome design](../pat-io-timing-oracle-design/DESIGN.md). A consistent,
independently checked trial is necessary evidence; it is not a complete group or

The closed combined matrix and preflight now exist as an isolated library.
All 25 named fixed challenges now have staged capture and independent outcome
components. The next implementation binds them to exact current-candidate
group execution, uses the new selected compiler identity within complete trusted
setup attestation, and enforces aggregate budgets
before any public qualification or promotion decision. The planner separates
candidate executions from fixed provider/authority challenges, with exact binding and budget associations for both. It refuses a case
count that exceeds the original limits. These components have not yet executed
as one exact current-candidate group or demonstrated compliance with its actual
aggregate evidence and wall-time limits.

Finite I/O qualification still does not implement persistent Arduino controllers,
natural-language synthesis/learning, physical driver providers, microscope
commissioning or intelligence-hub lifecycle/compute management. Those remain
part of the original end-to-end goal.
